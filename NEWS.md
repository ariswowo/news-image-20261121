## 1. Lead — Russia's barrage on Ukraine; missiles land in Poland

- Overnight 20–21 Nov, Russia fired 400+ drones and 110+ missiles at
  Ukraine (Ukraine said 404 drones, 84 cruise + 27 ballistic missiles);
  at least 8 people killed, 6 of them in Kyiv; three bridges across the
  Dnipro in Kyiv hit; power cut in parts of the capital.
- Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in south-east
  Poland (Nowy Lipowiec, Rudnik nad Sanem, Mołodycz); no injuries.
  PM Donald Tusk called it a deliberate provocation; NATO Secretary-General
  Mark Rutte called it a "grave violation" of Polish airspace.

## 3. Justice — ICJ: Myanmar committed genocide (ruled 19 Nov)

- On Thursday 19 November 2026 the International Court of Justice ruled
  9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its
  2016–2017 "clearance operations" — the first time the ICJ has found a
  state guilty of genocide — and ordered reparations, including
  facilitating the safe return of displaced Rohingya. About 10,000
  Rohingya were found to have been violently killed.
- Sources: UN Independent Investigative Mechanism for Myanmar
  (https://iimm.un.org/en/icj-judgment-marks-historic-step-towards-justice-rohingya);
